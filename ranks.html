A-rank reference

← Campaign checklist

Japanese version. Times are elapsed and phase-specific; aim below the threshold. A missing time means no separate cutoff was published, not an unlimited mission timer.

01 · Awakening

A rank: 10+ aircraft; unlock and complete the second wave.

Clock: 3:00 · Initial targets

Finish the four R-501 targets before 3:00 to trigger reinforcements. Take optional kills before the last red target.

JP mission guide

02 · Bravado

A rank: 23+ enemies, including the mission-update targets.

Clock: 4:00 · Initial radar sites

Destroy all eight radars by 3:59 to trigger the base targets. Optional aircraft and defenses count toward the total.

JP mission guide · Jerrold’s JP walkthrough

03 · Enter Dision

A rank: 5,000+ training points before the mission update.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Score based

The six practice targets give 1,800 points; earn at least 3,200 by following Dision closely. Then complete the container interception.

JP mission guide

04 · Paper Tiger

A rank: 23+ enemies and trigger the mission update.

Clock: 3:00 · Initial targets

Finish the first target group by 2:59. The faction decision occurs before the next normal save opportunity.

JP mission guide

05 · Broken Truce

A rank: 11+ enemies and complete the reinforcement phase.

Clock: 3:00 · Initial fighters

Destroy all four target F-15s by 2:59 to spawn the bombers. Slower completion gives Mission Over / D.

JP mission guide

06 · Ghosts of the Past

A rank: 8+ enemies and complete the secret-base objective.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Pursuit / discovery

Stay below the canyon rim and follow, rather than shoot, the recon plane. Destroy the discovered base; this opens No Clearance.

JP mission guide

07 · No Clearance

A rank: Follow Rena; trigger the second squadron and finish combat by the 5:00 mark.

Clock: 5:00 · Combat completion

Destroy the first four aircraft before 3:30 to spawn the six reinforcements. Returning to base opens Fragile Cargo but skips the A-rank combat clear.

JP mission guide

08 · Fragile Cargo

A rank: Protect the airship, then sink the escaping hydrofoil quickly.

Clock: 0:30 · From hydrofoil appearance

Clear the structures in the airship’s path. Sink the hydrofoil within 30 seconds after it appears.

JP mission guide

09 · Scylla and Charybdis

A rank: Shoot down all five initial target fighters.

Clock: 3:00 · Initial combat

Clear the initial five targets within three minutes. The subsequent choice sets the UPEO or Neucom route.

JP mission guide

10 · Fates Intertwined

A rank: Complete the mission.

Clock: 2:00 · Whole mission

Finish under two minutes. Intercept the high-altitude targets promptly.

JP mission guide

11 · Reaching for Stars

A rank: 18+ enemies; destroy every Antlion before landing.

Clock: 2:00 · Antlion phase

Destroy the ten Antlions in under two minutes to spawn reinforcements. Finish the additional targets after collecting enough kills.

JP mission guide

12 · One-Way Ticket

A rank: 10+ enemies; preserve the two neutral train cars.

Clock: 5:00 · Whole mission

Destroy the target train cars by 4:59. Use the escort aircraft to reach the required kill total.

JP mission guide

13 · Bug Hunt

A rank: Eradicate the nano-bites and disinfect the allied aircraft.

Clock: 4:30 · Whole mission

Use the special anti-nano-bite bombs; the final disinfection is part of the completion time.

JP mission guide

14 · Pawns in the Game

A rank: Destroy the armory without being detected by radar.

Clock: 9:00 · Whole mission

Finish under nine minutes. When jamming stops, climb above the indicated safe altitude before attacking again.

JP mission guide

15 · Damage Control

A rank: 22+ enemies; do not hit civilian aircraft.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill / protection based

Avoid the news helicopters. Finish both hostile waves after collecting enough optional kills.

JP mission guide

16 · Broken Wings

A rank: Shoot down all twelve hostile fighters.

Clock: 4:00 · Whole mission

The completion threshold is four minutes.

JP mission guide

17 · Sphyrna

A rank: Destroy all twelve optional fighters before finishing the carrier.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Nine XFA-36As and three R-103s must be destroyed in addition to the carrier’s target points.

JP mission guide

18 · A Canopy of Stars

A rank: 21+ enemies, then complete the final objective.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Collect optional kills before destroying the final escape aircraft.

JP mission guide

19 · Soldier of Fortune

A rank: 15+ enemies, including all grounded target aircraft.

Clock: 3:00 · Before aircraft takeoff

Finish the targets before three minutes. Letting them take off changes the outcome to Mission Over / D.

JP mission guide

20 · Megafloat

A rank: 24+ enemies; if the target hydrofoil launches, sink it.

Clock: 0:45 · From hydrofoil appearance

The hydrofoil phase starts at 3:30 if initial targets remain. Destroy the target boat within 45 seconds. Finishing before 3:30 instead leads to Partners.

JP mission guide

21 · Target Acquisition

A rank: Photograph all hangars in time and destroy 20+ enemies.

Clock: 3:00 · Photography phase

Complete all four hangar scans before three minutes to get the full reinforcements; the kill requirement spans the mission.

JP mission guide

22 · Partners

A rank: 18+ enemies and complete all objectives.

Clock: 5:00 · Whole mission

Destroy the power plants and exposed core before five minutes; missiles cannot guide here.

JP mission guide

23 · Tainted Peace

A rank: 9+ enemies, then complete the target interception.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Collect the optional kills before the final target ends the mission.

JP mission guide

24 · Stratosphere

A rank: 8+ enemies; complete the Mobura interception.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill / route based

Destroy the six R-531s plus at least two other enemies. Saving Keith instead produces a D-rank route to Welcoming Committee.

JP mission guide

25 · Welcoming Committee

A rank: Published requirement: 17+ enemies and intercept the shuttle before it lands.

Clock: 1:30 · Shuttle interception only

Destroy the R-808 by 1:29, then finish the remaining targets. The published enemy list and 17+ threshold do not fully reconcile; destroy every available hostile and verify the result screen.

JP mission guide · Jerrold’s JP walkthrough

26 · Technology Transfer

A rank: 12+ enemies; lose fewer than four Antlions.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Escort / escape based

Protect the Antlions, then shoot down the R-352 before it escapes. Losing every Antlion gives D.

JP mission guide

27 · Claustrophobia

A rank: 30+ enemies and complete the bombing objectives.

Clock: 6:00 · Whole mission

Finish before six minutes; a late missile-launch outcome gives Mission Over / D.

JP mission guide

28 · Dilemma

A rank: 30+ enemies and complete the dock attack.

Clock: 9:00 · Departure gate

Destroy the submarines and ships before their nine-minute departure. The final follow decision determines the ending route.

JP mission guide · Jerrold’s JP walkthrough

29 · Betrayal

A rank: 50+ destroyed targets and complete the mission.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Collect optional aircraft, ship weapons, and ground targets before finishing the last required objective.

JP mission guide

30 · Heart of the Serpent

A rank: 8+ enemies and inflict enough damage on the X-49.

Clock: 3:00 · From X-49 appearance

After the carrier phase, meet the X-49 damage threshold within three minutes. This opens Geofront Attack; being late skips it and gives D.

JP mission guide

31 · Geofront Attack

A rank: Complete the mission.

Clock: 4:00 · Whole mission

Finish under four minutes. This mission is reached by meeting the timed damage gate in Heart of the Serpent.

JP mission guide

32 · Casualties of War

A rank: 6+ enemies and complete both main targets.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Destroy enough of the optional fighter escorts before finishing the main targets.

JP mission guide

33 · Geopelia

A rank: Complete the remaining targets after the aircraft-control change.

Clock: 5:00 · From control change

The five-minute A-rank window starts when you take control of a Geopelia, not at the original mission start.

JP mission guide

34 · The Orientation

A rank: 29+ enemies and all three escaping target V-22Bs.

Clock: 1:30 · From helicopter update

Finish the escaping targets within 90 seconds for the A route. A separate replay leaving a target alive past 90 seconds opens Liquidation.

JP mission guide

35 · Liquidation

A rank: 37+ enemies; protect the carrier from the cruise missile.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill / interception based

Complete the submarine attack, then intercept the launched missile before impact.

JP mission guide

36 · Archnemesis

A rank: 21+ enemies and complete the final target.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Gather optional kills before destroying the escape aircraft.

JP mission guide

37 · Memory Error

A rank: Destroy all ten optional fighter targets and complete the main objectives.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

The optional group is three R-103s, two Su-43s, and five XFA-36As.

JP mission guide

38 · Electrosphere

A rank: Complete both phases of the mission.

Clock: 3:00 · Mission completion

The guide lists a three-minute completion threshold. Do not confuse this with the five-minute export-version requirement.

JP mission guide

39 · Power for Life

A rank: 21+ enemies, including all three oil tanks and four radar sites.

Clock: 4:00 · Initial RF-12A2 phase

Clear the initial pair in under four minutes. Destroy every oil tank and radar before the last main target for Guardian Angel. Leaving any of the seven intact produces D and opens Zero Gravity.

JP mission guide

40 · Guardian Angel

A rank: Protect the shuttle and shoot down the first two fighter squadrons.

Clock: 3:00 · Initial seven F-22Cs

All seven initial fighters must be down in under three minutes for A. Four minutes is the separate mission-update boundary.

JP mission guide

41 · Zero Gravity

A rank: Destroy all four satellites.

Clock: 2:30 · Whole combat phase

A-rank cutoff: 2:30. Hard failure occurs at 3:00. Complete the re-entry alignment afterward.

JP mission guide

42 · The Prize

A rank: 24+ enemies; at least one original recovery unit must survive.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Escort / arrival based

Keep the enemy ships away from the satellite and preserve part of the first recovery squadron.

JP mission guide

43 · Utopian Dreams

A rank: 12+ enemies and remain undetected by radar.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill / stealth based

Stay below the stated radar ceiling; collect optional ground targets before the last radar. The final decision chooses Fiona or Cynthia.

JP mission guide

44 · Reality Distortion

A rank: 31+ enemies and complete the mission.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Collect optional enemies as well as the required targets.

JP mission guide

45 · Counterrevolution

A rank: 23+ enemies and complete the mission.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Collect optional fighter kills before finishing the final main target.

JP mission guide

46 · Pursuit

A rank: Inflict the required damage and finish the mission.

Clock: 3:00 · Whole mission

Complete the damage objective in under three minutes.

JP mission guide

47 · Self Awareness

A rank: Complete the mission.

Clock: 5:30 · Whole mission

Finish under five minutes thirty seconds, including the generator phase.

JP mission guide

48 · Resistance

A rank: 17+ enemies and destroy all four carrier target points quickly.

Clock: 1:30 · From carrier mission update

Complete the four Sphyrna points within 90 seconds of the update to open Radio Silence. A late finish skips it and gives D.

JP mission guide

A rank: Complete the mission.

Clock: 4:00 · Whole mission

Finish under four minutes.

JP mission guide

50 · Revenge

A rank: Destroy all eleven enemies and complete the mission.

Clock: No separate cutoff published · Kill based

Clear the optional aircraft before the carrier’s last target point.

JP mission guide

51 · Tunnel Vision

A rank: Reach the end of the tunnel.

Clock: 4:00 · Whole mission

Complete the tunnel flight within four minutes. There are no hostile targets to farm.

Clock: 9:00 · Whole mission

Finish under nine minutes.

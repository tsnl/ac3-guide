Why this route is short

The target here is all 52 A ranks, all five endings, and their normal gameplay unlocks. This is a minimum-mission-entry route using normal after-mission saves; it does not claim minimum real-world speedrun time.

The full route takes 61 mission entries: all 52 missions plus nine repeated fork missions. After #02, 59 entries remain, provided #01 already has A. Nine fork missions must be revisited to cover both required outcomes while recording an A: 04, 07 (or repeat 06 instead), 09, 20, 24, 28, 34, 39, and 43. The count includes the short return-to-base replay of 07.

Several forks share one checkpoint: after 06 covers 07 and 09; after 09 covers 39 and 43; after 23 covers 24 and 28. The two detours that still need separate reloads are Megafloat and The Orientation. No long common segment is repeated.

Peak live checkpoints: S1 + S2, with S6 as a working save. Finished endings occupy the other slots. Final contents: S1 General Resource, S2 Ouroboros via General, S3 UPEO, S4 Neucom, S5 Ouroboros via Neucom, S6 working.

Save rules & what “100%” covers
  • Use the same in-game pilot/system data for every load. Save new results before loading an older checkpoint, including the system save after each ending.
  • Use S6 for routine saves after missions. Overwrite S1/S2 only when the plan says their pending branch has been covered.
  • 07, 24, 34, and 39 need a lower-grade branch visit as well as an A clear. The tracker preserves your best rank when you record these visits.
  • All checkmarks are your own log. This app does not read or modify an emulator save file.
  • “No separate cutoff listed” means the linked guide gives kills or objective conditions, not an unlimited mission timer. Timers are elapsed time; aim safely below a boundary.
  • The “after #02” preset records #02 as A and leaves #01’s rank unconfirmed. Confirm #01 in the guide; if it was not A, it needs an additional A clear.
  • The X-49 Night Raven’s special AppenDisc/deadcopy availability and third-party achievement challenges are outside ordinary campaign completion.
